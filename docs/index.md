# Privacy Policy for Dice Sim 3D

**Last updated: October 2026**

Dice Sim 3D is a 3D dice-rolling simulator for Android. This page explains what the
app does - and does not - do with your data.

## The short version

Dice Sim 3D collects no data of any kind. It has no internet access, no account
system, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything the app does happens entirely
on your device.

## What the app uses, and why

- **Motion sensors** (accelerometer / gravity sensor): used to detect when you shake
  or tilt your device, which drives the dice physics and camera on screen. These
  readings are processed in memory, in real time, and are never saved, logged, or
  sent anywhere.
- **App settings** (shake sensitivity, camera tilt on/off, roll history on/off):
  saved locally on your device using Android's standard app-preferences storage, so
  your choices persist between sessions. This data stays on your device.
- **Current dice, their colors, and your roll history**: held only in memory while
  the app is running, and cleared as soon as the app is closed. None of it is written
  to a file, backed up, or transmitted anywhere.

## No internet access

Dice Sim 3D does not request Android's internet permission and has no networking
code. It is not technically capable of sending any data - sensor readings, settings,
or anything else - to the developer or to any third party.

## Third parties

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software, and shares
no data with any third party, because it has no means of sharing data at all.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children, since it
does not collect personal data at all.

## Changes to this policy

If a future update to this app ever changes what data it collects or how, this page
will be updated to reflect that before the update is released.
